Ligatures

Ligatures in the italic only

The italic has two ligatures, fi and fl, in the standard liga feature, which browsers and most apps switch on by default. The roman has none: its f keeps the ball terminal and simply stands next to the i or l.

A serif made from two

Elanora keeps Lora’s generous x-height, its brushed curves and the wedge serifs that keep lines moving on a screen. Brygada 1918 lends it the ball terminals that hang from c, f, j, r and y like drops of ink, a few of its letters, and its figures: 1234567890.
